CENO STUDIO · FORM DATA · UPDATED 2026

What happens to the data you send in a project brief.

01 Who is responsible

Ceno Studio is operated by Ceno Labs Ltd. The studio is the only party that reads what you send through the intake form.

02 What is collected

Only the fields you fill in: your name, email address, project or company, what you are building, budget range and target timeline. Nothing else is collected and no tracking is used on the site.

03 What it is used for

One purpose: reading and replying to your enquiry. Your details are not added to any mailing list, not shared with third parties and not used for marketing.

04 How it is processed

Form submissions are relayed through a third-party form processor to the studio's mailbox. Outside that delivery step, the data stays with Ceno Labs Ltd and is not passed to anyone else.

05 How long it is kept

Enquiries are kept only as long as needed to handle the conversation, then deleted. Where an engagement follows, relevant material moves into that engagement's own records.

06 Your choices

You can ask what is held about you, ask for corrections or ask for deletion at any time — reply to any email from the studio, or send a note through the project intake form and it will be handled.
