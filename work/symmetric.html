SYMMETRIC FINANCE · MULTI-CHAIN DEFI · LIVE

A Balancer-based financial protocol, built and operated across multiple networks.

PROTOCOL ARCHITECTURE AMMS GOVERNANCE INCENTIVES MULTI-CHAIN SYSTEMS
01 Context

Symmetric runs Balancer-based AMM markets across several networks. The same financial system has to hold together everywhere at once: pool deployment, governance, emissions, incentives and the interfaces people actually use.

The protocol needed one senior owner for all of it — not a chain of handoffs between contractors.

02 Ceno's role

I built and operated the protocol end to end: protocol architecture, smart contracts, application engineering and production operations across networks.

The engagement extended into grant-funded development for Balancer and Tezos, and custom liquidity-locking infrastructure for Taiko.

03 What was built

The full protocol stack, delivered and operated as one system across networks.

  • Smart contracts — Balancer-based AMMs, pools and permissions
  • Governance, gauges, emissions and rewards
  • Incentive systems and liquidity programs
  • Subgraph indexing and data infrastructure
  • TypeScript SDKs for ecosystem integrations
  • Web frontends and application engineering
  • Multi-network deployments and operations
04 Technical depth
  • Multi-chain protocol ownership — one system, several networks
  • Balancer-weighted pool architecture as the financial core
  • Governance, gauge and emissions machinery end to end
  • Taiko 80/20 TAIKO/ETH liquidity lock-up and Trailblazers integration
  • Live migration and recovery work on production markets
05 Outcome

Symmetric is live and operating across multiple networks. The architecture later extended into supporting ecosystem work — Revolv's yield-accelerated pools, bribe-marketplace mechanics, lending integrations and additional token-economic mechanisms.
