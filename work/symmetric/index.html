Case study / Multi-chain DeFi

Symmetric Finance.

Built and operated a Balancer-based financial protocol across multiple networks — contracts to interface to production.

01 / Context

Newer networks needed credible, neutral liquidity infrastructure — not a fork-and-forget DEX, but a protocol that could host pools, incentives and governance from day one.

The challenge was standing up Balancer-grade AMM infrastructure on chains with thin tooling, then keeping it coherent as deployments, assets and stakeholders multiplied.

02 / Ceno's role

Ceno acted as protocol owner: architecture, smart-contract delivery, pool configuration, governance and incentives design, indexing, SDKs, frontend delivery and multi-chain deployment operations.

03 / What was built
  • Balancer-based AMM deployments across multiple networks, with weighted and managed pool configurations.
  • Governance, gauges, emissions and rewards flows directing incentives to productive liquidity.
  • Indexing (subgraphs), SDKs and frontends for pools, farms and governance.
  • Deployment and operations runbooks — each network instance launched and maintained as one system.
04 / Technical depth
  • Balancer Vault integration: pool factories, asset managers and rate providers as first-class infrastructure.
  • Gauge and incentive routing, with yield-accelerated pool variants explored via Revolv as an extension of the Symmetric architecture.
  • Custom liquidity-locking infrastructure for Taiko, including the 80/20 TAIKO/ETH lock-up and Trailblazers integration.
  • Grant-funded development for Balancer and Tezos, plus lending integrations and additional token-economic mechanisms.
  • Live migration and recovery experience: state, liquidity, permissions and integrations moved together.
05 / Outcome

Symmetric ran as production financial infrastructure across networks — contracts, incentives, analytics and interfaces maintained together. The same architecture later supported Revolv's yield-accelerated pools, bribe-marketplace work and lending integrations.
