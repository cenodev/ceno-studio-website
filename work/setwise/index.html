01 / Context

Tokenized assets are issued everywhere and tradeable almost nowhere coherent: fragmented issuers, inconsistent data, portfolio construction left to the user, execution scattered across venues.

Setwise treats that fragmentation as the protocol problem — discovery, product structure, market data and execution designed together.

02 / Ceno's role

Setwise is designed and built by Ceno as an original protocol: market modelling, routing and execution design, cross-chain settlement planning, governance shape and security controls.

03 / What is being built
  • Tokenized-asset discovery across issuers with normalized market data.
  • Portfolio-style products over tokenized assets.
  • Multi-source execution with routing and RFQ paths.
  • Cross-chain settlement design with explicit security controls.
04 / Technical depth
  • Multi-issuer market modelling: heterogeneous asset terms normalized into comparable structures.
  • Routing and RFQ execution across fragmented liquidity.
  • Cross-chain settlement with least-privilege permissions and recoverable flows.
  • Governance and security controls specified before launch, with internal review and independent audit preparation built into the plan.
