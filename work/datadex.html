DATADEX × VANA · ECOSYSTEM INFRASTRUCTURE · WITH THE VANA FOUNDATION

Exchange and liquidity infrastructure, purpose-built for the Vana ecosystem.

ECOSYSTEM INFRASTRUCTURE DEX ENGINEERING INDEXING ANALYTICS FULL-STACK DELIVERY
01 Context

The Vana Foundation needed exchange and liquidity infrastructure for the Vana blockchain — not a generic DEX reskin, but market plumbing designed around the ecosystem: its tokens, its data products and the people trading them.

Trading, liquidity and analytics all had to land together for the ecosystem to work.

02 Ceno's role

Built in collaboration with the Vana Foundation, I designed and delivered DataDex as a full-stack engagement: interface, market infrastructure and the data layer behind it.

The project is an ecosystem delivery story — infrastructure the Vana network could adopt, not a standalone product launch.

03 What was built

The exchange stack, delivered as one connected system for the Vana blockchain.

  • DEX interface — trading and pool management
  • Routing across ecosystem markets
  • Network integrations for the Vana blockchain
  • Subgraph indexing
  • Market analytics
04 Technical depth
  • Exchange interface engineered for Vana's token set
  • Liquidity management designed for concentrated pool positions
  • Local routing without reliance on external relayers
  • Subgraphs feeding market data and analytics
  • Full-stack delivery — chain, contracts and interface as one system
05 Outcome

DataDex is live, providing trading, liquidity and market infrastructure for the Vana blockchain — delivered as ecosystem infrastructure in collaboration with the Vana Foundation.
