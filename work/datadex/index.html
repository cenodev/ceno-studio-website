01 / Context

A new L1 lives or dies on whether people can trade, provide liquidity and read the market. Vana needed that foundation to be credible on day one — not a generic DEX deployment, but infrastructure tuned to its assets and users.

02 / Ceno's role

Built in collaboration with the Vana Foundation, Ceno delivered the exchange and liquidity layer: interface, pool management, routing, network integrations, indexing and market analytics — engineered as ecosystem infrastructure, not a standalone app.

03 / What was built
  • DEX trading interface with pool management and routing across Vana markets.
  • Network integrations for wallets, assets and bridging touchpoints.
  • Subgraphs indexing pools, tokens, volume and liquidity for fast, reliable reads.
  • Market analytics surface for tokens, pools and ecosystem activity.
04 / Technical depth
  • Routing tuned for Vana's early-liquidity conditions rather than deep-mainnet assumptions.
  • Subgraph-first market data: on-chain events normalized into queryable pool, token and volume entities.
  • Liquidity-management flows keeping pool configuration, incentives and interface consistent.
  • Full-stack delivery: contracts integration, indexing, SDK-shaped data access, frontend and deployment in one ownership line.
05 / Outcome

DataDex operates as Vana's trading and liquidity reference point — the place to swap, inspect pools and read the market — delivered as an ecosystem story, not simply another DEX.
